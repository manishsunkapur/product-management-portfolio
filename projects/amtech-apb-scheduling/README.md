# Amtech APB · Downtime Recovery

**Independent Product Manager / Product Owner work sample** for packaging operations scheduling.

> Concept only. The problem and feature gap are hypotheses; sample orders and timings are fictional. No Amtech customer research, product telemetry, or business results are claimed.

## Problem hypothesis

After an unexpected converting-line stop, a production scheduler may need a faster way to understand schedule changes, delivery risk, setup impact, and constraints before acting.

**Product goal:** Help planners confirm a feasible recovery schedule while protecting delivery commitments and schedule stability.

## What the concept tests

- Can a scheduler compare the current lineup with a proposed recovery?
- Are changed jobs, constraints, delivery risk, and setup trade-offs clear?
- Can the planner tell whether inputs are current and the schedule has been applied?
- Does the workflow handle stale inputs and no-feasible-plan cases safely?

The first prototype includes a rationale panel, apply/adjust/defer actions, and demo states for stale inputs and no feasible proposal. Multiple ranked options and automatic publication remain out of the initial scope until customer evidence supports them.
